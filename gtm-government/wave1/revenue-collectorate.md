- **1. Land Mutation & ROR** — mutation on succession/sale; RoR correction (missing survey nos, wrong classification, extent mismatch, Part-B); chain-of-title. Systems: Dharani/Bhu Bharati (TS), Webland+CCLA+MeeBhoomi (AP). Statute: TS Bhu Bharati Act 2025; AP RoR & Pattadar Pass Books Act 1971. AI: extract deed → check prior mutations/chain → verify deed-vs-RoR extent → draft mutation/correction order w/ risk flag. Highest sovereignty; direct Prajavani sibling.
- **2. Registration 22A/EC/Market-Value** — pre-registration deed scrutiny; Sec 22A prohibited-property block; EC generation; undervaluation detection. Systems: IGRS/CARD, e-STAMPS. Statute: Registration Act 1908 (17, 22A); Indian Stamp Act 1899. AI: extract deed → check 22A list + parse EC → verify consideration vs market value (stamp-leakage flag) → draft scrutiny/refusal note. **Cleanest, highest-feasibility, revenue-protection (CFO) pitch.**
- **3. Certificates** — caste/income/residence/EWS/integrated; very high volume (MeeSeva 300+, GSWS ~540 services). AI: extract + link family prior certs (auto-populate, catch fraud) → verify eligibility, surface missing docs before rejection → draft cert for MRO sign-off. **Fastest land-and-expand (feasibility 5).**
- **4. Collectorate Coordination** — review-meeting prep (TG-DMS: 87 params × 20 depts); VIP references; pendency escalation. AI: summarise dept feeds into review brief; rank SLA outliers; draft VIP acks + tasking letters. **Natural expansion from Prajavani (same Collector sponsor, prestige 5).**
- **5. Revenue Courts / RCCMS** — mutation appeals, boundary, assigned-land (POT), land-grabbing, NALA; big pendency. AI: extract petition → check prior orders/precedent → verify vs RoR → draft notices/cause-list/order skeleton. High ceiling, feasibility 3 (heavy HITL).
- **6-7:** Disaster Relief Enumeration (episodic, 15); Survey/Resurvey (GIS/drone-heavy, outside doc sweet-spot, 13).

**Co-leaders: #1 Land Mutation + #2 Registration-22A.** Fast expansion: #3 Certificates, #4 Collectorate.
